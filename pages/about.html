About This Project

What This Is
& Why It Exists

This site exists because of a simple, persistent truth that powerful people work very hard to obscure: the vast majority of people in the world have far more in common with each other than with those who govern, own, and decide on their behalf.

The nurse on a night shift in Manchester and the nurse on a night shift in Manila face the same exhaustion, the same inadequate pay, the same impossible moral burden of caring for people in systems built to profit rather than heal. The farmworker in California and the farmworker in India both live closer to the margins than the corporations that depend on their labor. The single parent in Atlanta and the single parent in Lagos share sleepless nights over the same questions: rent, food, school, the future.

None of that means our struggles are identical. They aren't. Context matters enormously. History matters. But the manufactured hatreds — the nationalism, the xenophobia, the culture wars designed to make you fear the stranger — these serve a purpose. They keep us looking sideways instead of up.

What We Believe

We believe in mutual aid — not as charity from the fortunate to the unfortunate, but as the practice of communities recognizing their interdependence and acting on it. The word "mutual" means something: it flows in all directions. Everyone has something to give. Everyone has something they need.

We believe that the best solutions to human problems have almost always come from communities organizing themselves — not waiting for institutions or leaders to act, but building the structures they needed themselves. Free schools, mutual aid societies, food networks, care cooperatives, tenant unions: the history of working-class survival is a history of collective self-organization.

We believe that division is manufactured, and that recognizing it is the first act of resistance. This doesn't mean every conflict is artificial — real power disparities exist, and they cause real harm. But the framing that makes an immigrant your enemy instead of your potential ally? That's a product. It's sold to you deliberately.

What This Site Is Not

This is not a political party, not affiliated with any government, not funded by any foundation or corporation. There is no leadership structure here, no board of directors, no official spokesperson.

It's a platform for ideas, tools, resources, and analysis that might be useful to people trying to build something better wherever they are. Copy freely. Share widely. Use everything here however it helps you.
