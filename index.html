The Core Truth

They Want You
To Fear Your Neighbor

The same strategies used by every ruling class in history: divide, distract, and conquer. Pointing fingers sideways so you never look up.

01

We Are Not Each Other's Enemies

The nurse in Brazil, the factory worker in Poland, the farmer in Nigeria — they share more with you than any billionaire, general, or politician ever will. Economic insecurity, bad housing, climate fear, overwork: these are shared conditions, not accidents.

02

Mutual Aid Is Power

Not charity. Not pity. Mutual aid is people looking out for each other because we understand our fates are linked. When you help your neighbor move, share food, or organize care — you're practicing the only politics that actually lasts.

03

Community Builds What States Won't

Governments respond to crises after the fact — communities respond immediately. Neighbors brought food during the pandemic. Neighbors shared generators after storms. Neighbors organized rides to hospitals. This is the infrastructure that actually works.

04

Borders Are Tools of Division

Lines drawn by conquerors, maintained by states, enforced against the very people they claim to protect. The worker on one side of the line and the worker on the other are far more alike than either is to those who drew it.

05

Small Actions Compound

You don't need to overthrow anything today. Show up for your neighbors. Share skills. Give time. Pool resources. History changes because enough ordinary people made enough small decisions to trust each other instead of their rulers.

06

The Future Is Not Given

Every oppressive system that seemed permanent eventually ended — not because it collapsed on its own, but because enough people decided the cost of action was lower than the cost of waiting. We are in that kind of moment. Act accordingly.
