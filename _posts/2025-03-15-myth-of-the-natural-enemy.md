Every ruling class in history has required that ordinary people fear each other more than they fear the people ruling them. This is not an accident. It is the foundational technology of control.

## The Pattern

Look at how it works. In the United States, poor white workers in the 19th century were convinced that their precarity was caused by freed Black workers — not by the plantation owners and industrial capitalists who set the wages, owned the land, and controlled the courts. In Britain, working class anger about housing and wages was redirected toward immigrants, toward Muslims, toward "scroungers" — anyone except the landlords, the bankers, and the politicians who deregulated everything and extracted everything.

This pattern repeats in every country, every era. The specific group changes. The immigrants, the religious minority, the foreign power, the "welfare queens," the "globalists." The mechanism is identical.

**Give people an enemy they can see across the street, and they'll never look for the enemy in the boardroom.**

## Why It Works

The story is emotionally compelling. It has a clear villain at human scale. It confirms anxieties that are real — the anxiety about housing, about wages, about your children's future — and provides a concrete, visible target. The actual causes of those anxieties are abstract: financialization, regulatory capture, the lobbying complex, compound interest on debt. These are hard to see and hard to fight.

Your neighbor? You can see your neighbor. Your neighbor is easy to resent.

## The Counter

The counter to manufactured division is not simply saying "we're all the same." We're not all the same. Our material situations differ. Our histories differ. The harm done by racism, colonialism, and patriarchy is real and ongoing — pretending it isn't is its own form of manipulation.

The counter is building enough trust across those lines that the manufactured panic doesn't take hold. When you actually know people across the lines you're supposed to fear — when you've eaten together, worked together, helped each other through hard things — the fear campaign loses its grip. Not completely. But meaningfully.

**Mutual aid is not just practically useful. It is structurally anti-fascist.** It builds the cross-cutting relationships that make division harder to sell.

## Start There

You don't need a theory of everything. You need relationships that cross the lines you've been told are impermeable. Start with whoever is near you. Build trust. Do it before the next crisis requires it.

The crisis is already here. Start anyway.
